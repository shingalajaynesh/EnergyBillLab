1. **University Sustainability & Energy Extension Offices:** State land-grant university energy extension resource pages.
2. **Home Energy Efficiency Nonprofits:** Local Weatherization Assistance Program (WAP) operators, community action agencies, and energy efficiency educational portals.
3. **HVAC & Building Science Publications:** Consumer-facing home improvement, HVAC installation, and green building blogs with strict editorial standards.
4. **Utility & Personal Finance Journalists:** Energy and utility consumer reporters covering electricity rate hikes and household cost diagnostics.
5. **Open Data & Calculator Directories:** Educational utility directories and home energy tool indexes.
