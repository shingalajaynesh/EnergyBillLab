Original calculation:

- April 2026 EIA residential price: $18.17/Mcf.
- April 2025 comparison price: $16.17/Mcf.
- Year-over-year change: $2.00/Mcf, about 12.4%.
- EIA unit conversion: 1,000 cubic feet * 1,036 Btu/cubic foot = 1,036,000 Btu. 1 therm = 100,000 Btu, so 1 Mcf = about 10.36 therms.
- April 2026 price per therm: $18.17 / 10.36 = about $1.75/therm.
- April 2025 price per therm: $16.17 / 10.36 = about $1.56/therm.
- Difference: about $0.19/therm.
